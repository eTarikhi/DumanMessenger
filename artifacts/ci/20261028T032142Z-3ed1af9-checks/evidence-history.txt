For scale: a 20-node campus sends ~2.8k relay transmissions in 300 s of ordinary traffic,
so a five-member group's beacons are noise next to the routing plane they ride on.

Beacons go to co-members in range and nowhere else, so what anti-entropy repairs is the
last hop of a loss — a member that was asleep, out of range or switched off. It cannot
bridge a membership that cannot hear itself: that is the scoped flood's job, and the
spread arm above is the measurement of where the two responsibilities meet.
