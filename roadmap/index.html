Risks

What kills mesh apps — and Duman’s answers

iOS
Problem: BG BLE throttled, no WiFi Direct, no BLE-Mesh node.
Answer: Ship Android-first; iOS = companion (document BLE-only interop).
Voice
Problem: Multi-hop latency >1s → no duplex.
Answer: Market as push-to-talk; stream only 1-hop WiFi; else voice-note.
Range
Problem: Sparse = no relay = dead.
Answer: DTN OppQueue (“will send when someone passes”) + community SUPERs.
Battery
Problem: Briar drains 4× vs server apps.
Answer: NORMAL duty-cycle 3s/7s; SUPER opt-in + charging bonus.
Takedown
Problem: Stores can delist (India’26).
Answer: APK + F-Droid + GitHub; mesh works even after delist.
Fragment
Problem: Huawei no-GMS, vendor BLE quirks.
Answer: Test raw BLE+WiFi fallback by W6; no Nearby dependency wall.
