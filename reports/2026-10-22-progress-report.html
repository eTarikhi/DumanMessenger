The most consequential finding of the window. A render smoke test for the new SOS card reported “20 of 19 online nodes heard”. The impossible denominator traced to a real protocol defect: a node accepted copies of its own message relayed back by peers, treated them as news, delivered them to itself and re-flooded them — a storm amplifier on the one message type that already floods to every peer. Fixing it cut relay transmissions 18.3% in the warm campus world and 4.0% in the 60-node crowd, and lifted delivery 99.3 → 99.7% and 84.7 → 86.2% across six seeds. It also invalidated one acceptance pin that had been measuring the amplifier rather than MPR, which is now re-pinned on a six-seed mean with the reasoning recorded in the test.

Open gaps, stated plainly.
  • 60-node crowd, delivery within 5 hops: 77.5% against a 90% bar. The tail is messages at exactly 6 hops against a data TTL of 5, on a geometry whose clusters sit 61–74 m apart with a 31.6 m LOS hop — a 3-hop floor before anyone moves. Raising ogmTtl and raising data TTL were both tried and falsified. D4 is the scoped attempt; if it does not close, the honest outcome is a documented geometric floor plus a density recommendation, not a fudged number.
  • Always-scanning NORMAL battery: 19.4%/8 h against skills/07’s <15%. The duty cycle brings it to 10.0–10.9% and is measured delivery-neutral, so the sleep slot is load-bearing. It ships as an opt-in flag with the gate applied only under --duty-cycle, and the always-on miss is documented rather than hidden.
  • Scale above 60 nodes is untested although skills/08 asks for 10–200 and the engine has no structural limit. D3 closes it.
  • No real radio. Every figure here comes from a simulator whose RF model is a distance-plus-walls path loss with a margin-based loss curve. It is calibrated and internally consistent, and it is not a measurement of Bluetooth. Phase 2’s field test (D9) is the item that can confirm or falsify all of it, and it needs 15 handsets.
  • E2EE, CRDT sync, Wi-Fi Direct and the audio pipeline are specified but unbuilt. They are the research phase (D5–D8) and the largest remaining block of the roadmap.

Trajectory, in one line. The protocol core the roadmap depends on — BATMAN-lite scoring and election, per-neighbour link quality, MPR suppression, spray-and-wait DTN, duty-cycled power, SOS flooding and hop transparency — now exists twice, agrees bit-for-bit, is gated by CI on every commit, and meets every documented efficiency and stability target with large margins. What remains is either research (crypto, transport, sync), density scale, or hardware this environment does not have. Momentum is on the research block next.
